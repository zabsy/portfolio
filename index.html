About me

I'm an engineer based out of Waterloo and Toronto, focused on physical AI and autonomous robotics. Over the past few years I've worked on robots from competition bots to humanoids, across everything from software and hardware to autonomy and AI. Right now I'm building humanoid robots with WATonomous.

What motivates me most is seeing engineering make a positive impact on people's lives, especially in medtech. I previously worked on AI/ML for medical imaging at Ramsoft. I also love photography, sports, and reading.
